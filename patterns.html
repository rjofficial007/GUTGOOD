Pattern detail screens · all six, colour-themed

Each screen takes its pattern’s colour across the whole surface — background wash, app bar, section markers, chips, metric numerals and the CTA all derive from the accent, so the screen reads as belonging to that pattern. Anatomy mirrors the real InsightBentoPattern: repeating-episode hero (trigger → reaction + confidence), tick-fan metrics (episodes = frequency / totalSimilarMeals, onset lag, confidence = evidenceRatio), biological root, swap pairs, occurrences, common factors and the recommendation.
